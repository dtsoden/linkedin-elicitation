Every bookkeeper has had the client who wants the books closed by the 3rd and sends receipts on the 9th.

Month-end close for a typical client takes our team 9 to 16 hours. The ones near 9 send bank access once and leave it alone. Everyone near 16 has a card the owner uses for groceries.

I suspect most firms our size land in that range. The firms well below it have figured out something about intake that I'd like to steal.

For the record, the grocery card is always a Costco card.
